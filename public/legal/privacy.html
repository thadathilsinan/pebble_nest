Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Pebble is a day planner made by Moduluz.io ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information Pebble collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Pebble apps and to the Pebble service they connect to.

The short version

  • We collect your email address, an optional name, your preferences, and the blocks, tasks, and notes you create — only what Pebble needs to work.
  • We do not sell or rent your information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We share information only with the providers that run Pebble for us (hosting, database, email delivery) and with Google or Apple when you choose to sign in with them.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time. That permanently deletes your account and everything in it.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information. Your email address, which you use to sign in, and your name if you add one or if it comes from Google or Apple when you sign in with them.
  • Preferences. Your settings, such as the day your week starts on, 12- or 24-hour time, and your time zone. The app reads your time zone from your device so that your days begin and end at the right time.
  • Your planning content. The blocks you create (their names, times, and repeat schedules), the tasks inside them (titles, notes, dates, and reminder times), and what happens to them — completed, skipped, carried over to another day, or missed. Pebble keeps this history so it can show you your review and dashboard.
  • Messages to us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include.

Information created when you use Pebble

  • Sign-in and session records. When you sign in, we record how you signed in (email code, Google, or Apple) and create a session so you stay signed in. Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that cannot be turned back into the original.
  • Server logs. Like almost every internet service, our servers record basic technical details of each request the app makes: your IP address, the time, the address requested, and whether it succeeded. We use these logs to keep Pebble running, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse. They do not contain your password-equivalent tokens or the content of your blocks and tasks.

Information from Google or Apple

If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, see section 6 for what we receive.

2. Information we do not collect

Pebble does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, health data, payment details, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking tools, and this page sets no cookies.

Reminders are scheduled on your device using its built-in notification system. We do not use a push notification service and do not collect device push tokens.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Provide Pebble — create and maintain your account, sign you in, store and sync your blocks and tasks, carry unfinished tasks forward, schedule your reminders, and show your review.
  • Send sign-in codes — when you sign in with email, we send a one-time code to your address. We do not send marketing email.
  • Keep Pebble secure and working — prevent abuse (for example, limiting how often sign-in codes can be requested), investigate problems, and fix bugs.
  • Respond to you — answer your questions and requests.
  • Meet legal obligations — comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not build profiles about you, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example, under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK), we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the service you signed up for: your account, your planning content, sign-in, and reminders.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep Pebble secure, prevent abuse, and diagnose problems using server logs. These interests are not overridden by your rights, because the data involved is minimal and kept only briefly.
  • Legal obligation — where we must keep or disclose information to comply with the law.
  • Consent — where you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, or where local law (such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) treats your use of the service as consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

6. Signing in with Google or Apple

Google. When you sign in with Google, Google confirms your identity to us and shares your email address and your name. We use your email address to find or create your Pebble account and, if your account does not yet have a name, we use the name from Google. We do not request access to your Google contacts, calendar, files, or any other Google data. Pebble's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Apple. When you sign in with Apple, Apple shares your email address — or, if you choose "Hide My Email", a private relay address that forwards to you — and, the first time only, your name. Apple also gives us a token that we keep so that, if you delete your Pebble account, we can tell Apple to remove Pebble's access to your Apple ID.

Google and Apple handle your information under their own privacy policies. You can remove Pebble's access at any time from your Google Account or Apple ID settings.

7. How long we keep your information

  • Your account and planning content — for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, it and everything in it are permanently deleted from our database straight away.
  • Backups — our database provider keeps short-term recovery copies so that data can be restored after a failure. Deleted data disappears from these as they are replaced by newer copies, after a limited period.
  • Sessions — end when you sign out, and expire after 60 days without use.
  • Sign-in codes — work only once and expire 10 minutes after they are sent.
  • Server logs — kept in rotating files that are overwritten as new logs are written, normally within a few weeks. We do not archive them.
  • Emails you send us — for as long as needed to deal with your request.

We may keep information longer only where the law requires it.

8. How we protect your information

We protect your information with measures appropriate to its sensitivity, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers, and between our servers and our database;
  • storing sign-in codes and session tokens only in hashed form;
  • keeping your sign-in credentials on your device in its secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android);
  • short-lived access tokens, limits on sign-in attempts, and restricting access to our systems to the people who run Pebble.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Where your information is processed

Moduluz.io is based in India. Our service providers operate data centres in other countries, so your information may be stored and processed outside the country where you live, in countries whose data protection laws may differ from yours. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses offered by our providers.

10. Your choices and rights

Whatever country you live in, you can:

  • See and change your information — your name, preferences, blocks, and tasks can be viewed and edited in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account — in the app, go to You → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it, signs you out everywhere, and, if you used Sign in with Apple, revokes Pebble's access to your Apple ID. It cannot be undone.
  • Get a copy of your information — email us and we will send you a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us anything else — including to correct, restrict, or object to how we use your information, or to withdraw consent.

To make a request, email hello@moduluz.io from the address on your account, so that we can confirm the request is yours. We will respond within 30 days, or sooner where the law requires. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

United States, including California

We disclose this information only to the service providers listed in section 5. We do not sell or "share" (for cross-context behavioural advertising) personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information. Depending on your state, you have the right to know about, access, correct, delete, and obtain a copy of your personal information, and to appeal if we decline a request — email us at hello@moduluz.io, and you may use an authorized agent. Pebble does not respond to Do Not Track signals because it does not track you.

12. Children

Pebble is not directed at children, and you must be at least 13 years old — or the minimum age required by the law where you live, if that is older — to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy as Pebble changes or the law requires. We will change the effective date at the top, and if a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the app or by email at least 7 days before it takes effect.
